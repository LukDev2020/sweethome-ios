Beaconify keeps the people you love one tap away. When someone needs help, the family they chose hears about it right away, with where they are.

Made for parents who live alone, kids coming home late, riders on night shifts and family members travelling abroad.

SOS TO YOUR FAMILY
Press and hold the SOS button, or use a Bluetooth panic button, the widget or Siri. A short countdown stops accidental alerts. Your guardians get an alert with your location; if nobody answers, Beaconify follows up by text and phone call. You see who has been reached and who is on the way.

FALL AND DISTRESS DETECTION
Optional fall detection asks "Are you OK?" and alerts family if you don't answer within 60 seconds. Voice SOS and distress-sound detection listen on your phone only.

LOCATION, ONLY FOR THE PEOPLE YOU CHOOSE
Share your location with the guardians you approve — and choose, for each one, what they can see. Safe zones, arrival reports and a home timer that tells family if you're late.

CHECK-INS
One tap to say you're fine. If a check-in is missed, your guardians are told.

MORE
- Medical card for first responders (blood type, allergies, medications)
- Trip recording with route and export
- Private family feed for photos and updates
- World clocks for family across time zones
- Duress code and discreet modes for difficult situations
- Lock-screen widgets and Live Activities
- English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Français, 日本語

PRIVACY
No ads and no tracking. Your location is shared only with guardians you approve. Pause sharing any time, and delete your account and data from Settings.

PLANS
SOS, fall detection, check-ins and live location are free. Optional plans add more guardians and family members, longer history and reports. Monthly and yearly plans start with a 3-day free trial; payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings.

IMPORTANT
Beaconify is not an emergency service and does not contact 911 or other emergency numbers. In danger, call your local emergency number first.
