Review Notes for Apple App Review Team

1. APP PURPOSE
Beaconify (守灯 in Chinese) is a family safety app. It connects people being looked after ("Protected") with family members who look after them ("Guardians"). It provides an SOS alert to family, location sharing with family the person approves, check-ins, a home-arrival timer, and a medical card.

2. TRYING THE APP
- Without an account: on the sign-in screen tap "Try a demo first" for a guided demo with a sample family. Nothing is sent to real people.
- Test account (sign in with phone):

  SMS code: 123456
  Role: Guardian, with one linked protected person.
- Sign in with Apple is offered next to phone sign-in. "Sign in with Alipay" is also offered for users in China.
- The features in section 10 are on the Protected side: Settings > switch role to "Protected" (or use the demo).

3. SOS
- The red SOS button sends an alert to the person's own guardians (push, then SMS and a voice call if nobody responds), with their location.
- It does NOT contact 911 or any emergency service. The onboarding and the SOS screen say so: "Beaconify is not an emergency service. In danger, call your local emergency number first."
- A short cancel countdown prevents accidental alerts.

4. LOCATION
- Shared only with the guardians the person has approved, who can be limited per guardian (location can be hidden from any of them).
- "Always" permission keeps safe-zone and check-in alerts working in the background; sharing can be paused in Settings.
- Never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with other third parties.

5. BACKGROUND MODES
- location: safe-zone monitoring, location for family during an SOS, trip recording the person starts.
- remote-notification: SOS alerts, check-in reminders, silent heartbeat checks.
- fetch: the "check-in timer" (safety timer / dead-man switch) — a BGAppRefreshTask (com.shoudeng.deadman.check) that lets the phone send the SOS if the person doesn't check in by the time they set. The deadline is also kept on our server, which sends the SOS itself if the phone is off or the app was closed.
- bluetooth-central: an optional Bluetooth panic button or tag the person pairs in Settings (press to send SOS; alert when a tag is left behind). Used only after pairing.

6. PERMISSIONS
- Microphone and speech recognition: only if the person turns on voice SOS ("help" / "救命") or distress-sound detection. Recognition and sound classification run on the device; no audio is recorded or uploaded.
- Motion: optional fall detection (60-second "Are you OK?" countdown before family is alerted).
- Contacts: only the contact the person picks with the system contact picker, to add as an emergency contact.
- Photos: photos the person chooses to post to their private family feed.

7. SUBSCRIPTIONS
- In-app purchases (6): Single and Family, each monthly / yearly / lifetime.
- "Organization" plans for care homes and schools are not sold in the app: the plan page shows a "Contact us" email button only (an enterprise service agreed outside the app, 3.1.3(c)).
- SOS, check-ins and one guardian work without paying; no paywall blocks basic safety features.
- Price, period and auto-renewal are shown before purchase, with Terms of Use (Apple EULA) and Privacy Policy links; Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings.

8. HEALTH DATA
- The medical card (blood type, allergies, medications) is typed in by the person. No HealthKit.

9. ACCOUNT DELETION
- Settings > Delete Account, for both roles. It deletes the account and its data on our servers and revokes Sign in with Apple.

10. DISCREET SAFETY FEATURES (for people in danger — all optional, off by default, Protected > Settings)
- Disguise mode: makes the app open to a working calculator, weather or notes screen so an abuser glancing at the phone doesn't see a safety app. The app's icon and name don't change. To get back to the app: Calculator — type 911 then "="; Weather — triple-tap the temperature; Notes — type the secret phrase the person set. It can be turned off in the same Settings screen.
- Duress code: a secret code the person sets. If someone forces them to end an SOS, entering this code closes the SOS screen as if it ended, while the SOS keeps alerting their guardians.
- Silent alarm: scheduled check-ins; if the person misses the number of check-ins they chose, their guardians are alerted with the last known location.
- Safety timer: a countdown the person starts (e.g. walking home); if they don't check in before it ends, their guardians get an SOS.
- Fake call: shows an incoming-call screen (CallKit) with a caller name the person picks, so they can leave an uncomfortable situation. No real call is placed or received and nothing is dialled; optionally an SOS is sent when the fake call ends.
- Lock-screen card (Live Activity, optional, Settings > Lock screen card): SOS (opens the app's 5-second cancel countdown), Silent SOS (needs two taps within 5 seconds; sends the SOS without showing anything on screen), Fake call (as above), and "OK" check-in. During a safety timer it shows the time left with "+15 min" and "I'm here". During an SOS it shows which guardians were reached and, if the person doesn't open the app for two minutes (or right after a fall), the medical details the person chose to show plus buttons to call the local emergency number or a family member — for whoever finds the phone.
None of these contact emergency services by themselves (a call to the emergency number is only placed when someone taps it and confirms the system call prompt); all alerts go only to the person's own guardians.
