Review Notes for Apple App Review Team

1. APP PURPOSE
Beaconify (守灯 in Chinese) is a family safety app. It connects people being looked after ("Protected") with family members who look after them ("Guardians"). It provides an SOS alert to family, location sharing with family the person approves, check-ins, a home-arrival timer, and a medical card.

2. TRYING THE APP
- Without an account: on the sign-in screen tap "Try a demo first" for a guided demo with a sample family. Nothing is sent to real people.
- Test account (sign in with phone):

  SMS code: 123456
  Role: Guardian, with one linked protected person.
- Sign in with Apple is offered next to phone sign-in.

3. SOS
- The red SOS button sends an alert to the person's own guardians (push, then SMS and a voice call if nobody responds), with their location.
- It does NOT contact 911 or any emergency service. The onboarding and the SOS screen say so: "Beaconify is not an emergency service. In danger, call your local emergency number first."
- A short cancel countdown prevents accidental alerts.

4. LOCATION
- Shared only with the guardians the person has approved, who can be limited per guardian (location can be hidden from any of them).
- "Always" permission keeps safe-zone and check-in alerts working in the background; sharing can be paused in Settings.
- Never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with other third parties.

5. BACKGROUND MODES
- location: safe-zone monitoring, location for family during an SOS, trip recording the person starts.
- remote-notification: SOS alerts, check-in reminders, silent heartbeat checks.
- fetch: the "check-in timer" (dead-man switch) — a BGAppRefreshTask (com.shoudeng.deadman.check) that sends the SOS if the person doesn't check in by the time they set.
- bluetooth-central: an optional Bluetooth panic button or tag the person pairs in Settings (press to send SOS; alert when a tag is left behind). Used only after pairing.

6. PERMISSIONS
- Microphone and speech recognition: only if the person turns on voice SOS ("help" / "救命") or distress-sound detection. Recognition and sound classification run on the device; no audio is recorded or uploaded.
- Motion: optional fall detection (60-second "Are you OK?" countdown before family is alerted).
- Contacts: only the contact the person picks with the system contact picker, to add as an emergency contact.
- Photos: photos the person chooses to post to their private family feed.

7. SUBSCRIPTIONS
- Plans: Single, Family (monthly / yearly / lifetime) and Ultimate (monthly / yearly).
- SOS, check-ins and one guardian work without paying; no paywall blocks basic safety features.
- Price, period and auto-renewal are shown before purchase, with Terms of Use (Apple EULA) and Privacy Policy links; Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings.

8. HEALTH DATA
- The medical card (blood type, allergies, medications) is typed in by the person. No HealthKit.

9. ACCOUNT DELETION
- Settings > Delete Account, for both roles. It deletes the account and its data on our servers and revokes Sign in with Apple.
