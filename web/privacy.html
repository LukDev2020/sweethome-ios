隐私政策

一、我们收集哪些信息

  • 位置信息：用于安全区检测、紧急求助定位、行动轨迹记录。仅在授权后采集，仅与您指定的守护者共享。
  • 运动传感器数据：用于跌倒检测。数据仅在本地处理，不上传服务器。
  • 手机号码：用于注册账号及紧急短信通知。
  • 设备信息：电量、在线状态等，用于守护者了解被守护设备是否正常运行。
  • 姓名与头像：用于家庭圈内成员识别。
  • 通讯录：仅在您主动添加紧急联络人时读取，不会后台扫描或上传完整通讯录。
  • 照片：仅在您主动分享到家庭圈时使用，照片仅对守护关系内的家人可见。
  • 医疗信息：血型、过敏史、用药等，由您手动输入，用于紧急情况下供守护者参考。

二、我们如何使用信息

  • 紧急求助：触发 SOS 时向守护者发送您的位置。
  • 安全监测：检测异常（跌倒、失联）并及时通知守护者。
  • 报平安：让守护者了解您的安全状态。

三、数据保留

  • 免费用户：位置数据保留 7 天，签到记录保留 30 天。
  • 付费用户：位置数据保留 90 天，签到记录永久保留。
  • SOS 事件记录及知情同意审计日志永久保留。

四、数据共享

  • 您的位置与安全信息仅与您明确授权的守护者共享。
  • 我们不会将您的个人信息出售给任何第三方。
  • 紧急分享链接仅在您或系统自动升级时创建，有效期不超过 48 小时。

五、数据安全

  • 所有数据传输使用 TLS 加密。
  • 敏感凭证存储在 iOS 钥匙串（Keychain）中。
  • 服务器端数据静态加密。

六、您的权利

  • 访问权：在设置中随时导出您的所有数据。
  • 删除权：在设置中删除账号，将永久删除所有数据。
  • 撤回同意：随时在系统设置中关闭位置或运动传感器权限。

七、联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请联系：privacy@shoudeng.app

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

  • Location: Used for safe-zone detection, emergency SOS positioning, and movement trajectory recording. Collected only with your authorization and shared only with your designated guardians.
  • Motion Sensor Data: Used for fall detection. Processed locally on your device; not uploaded to our servers.
  • Phone Number: Used for account registration and emergency SMS notifications.
  • Device Information: Battery level, online status, etc., so guardians can monitor whether the protected device is functioning normally.
  • Name & Profile Photo: Used for member identification within the family circle.
  • Contacts: Read only when you actively add an emergency contact. We never scan or upload your full address book in the background.
  • Photos: Used only when you share to the family feed. Photos are visible only to family members within a guardianship relationship.
  • Medical Information: Blood type, allergies, medications, etc. Manually entered by you, available to guardians for reference in emergencies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Emergency Assistance: Sending your location to guardians when SOS is triggered.
  • Safety Monitoring: Detecting anomalies (falls, loss of connectivity) and promptly notifying guardians.
  • Check-Ins: Letting guardians know you are safe.

3. Data Retention

  • Free users: Location data retained for 7 days; check-in records for 30 days.
  • Paid users: Location data retained for 90 days; check-in records retained permanently.
  • SOS event records and consent audit logs are retained permanently.

4. Data Sharing

  • Your location and safety information are shared only with guardians you have explicitly authorized.
  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party.
  • Emergency share links are created only when you or the system escalates automatically and are valid for no more than 48 hours.

5. Data Security

  • All data transmissions use TLS encryption.
  • Sensitive credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Server-side data is encrypted at rest.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Export all your data anytime from Settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account from Settings; all data will be permanently removed.
  • Withdraw Consent: Disable location or motion sensor permissions anytime in system settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@shoudeng.app.
