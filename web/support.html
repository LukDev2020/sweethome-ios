Common questions · 常见问题

  • My family doesn't get my SOS. Make sure they accepted your invite and allow Beaconify notifications. In Settings › Location choose “Always”.
    家人收不到求助：请确认对方已接受邀请并允许守灯通知；定位权限请选「始终」。
  • Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Beaconify.
    取消订阅：iPhone「设置 › 你的名字 › 订阅 › 守灯」。
  • Restore a purchase. In the app: Settings › Plans & Billing › Restore Purchases.
    恢复购买：App 内「设置 › 方案与订阅 › 恢复购买」。
  • Delete my account and data. In the app: Settings › Delete Account. This removes your account and the data we hold about you.
    删除账户和数据：App 内「设置 › 删除账户」。
